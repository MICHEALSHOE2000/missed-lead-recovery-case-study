Services & skills

How I help
you sell.

Paid advertising
I plan and run Google, Meta and TikTok ads around the offer, the buyer and what the business can afford to spend to win a customer.
Sales funnels & landing pages
I write the offer and design the page people land on after the click. For a store, that means a clear path to checkout. For a service business, it means a qualified enquiry and a booked call.
Tracking & follow-up
I connect GA4, GTM and TikTok Pixel to the funnel, then use CRM and AI automation to help the sales team follow up and trace closed sales back to the campaign.

My process

How I work.

  1. Start with the numbers

    I ask what a sale is worth, what margin is left and how buyers decide. That gives us a target acquisition cost before we spend.

  2. Match the ad to the offer

    I carry the promise from the ad onto the landing page and give the buyer one clear next step.

  3. Follow the sale

    I review which clicks became orders or qualified leads, where buyers dropped off and what to test next.
