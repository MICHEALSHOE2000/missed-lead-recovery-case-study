My process

How I work.

  1. Start with the numbers

    I ask what a sale is worth, what margin is left and how buyers decide. That gives us a target acquisition cost before we spend.

  2. Match the ad to the offer

    I carry the promise from the ad onto the landing page and give the buyer one clear next step.

  3. Follow the sale

    I review which clicks became orders or qualified leads, where buyers dropped off and what to test next.
