The architecture

One journey. Eight connected layers.

The same lead ID and attribution data move forward at every step, so the owner can see which campaign produced a customer—not just a form fill.

Google Ad

Intent-matched campaign with UTM and click ID.

Qualification

Auditable fit score, explanation, and sales route.

06FU

07BK

Revenue
